🧸 How to make your squishy

✂️

1. Cut

Cut the foam into your favorite shape.

🧱

2. Mold

Add clay and shape your squishy.

🌬️

3. Dry

Wait for your squishy to dry.

🎨

4. Paint

Draw and decorate your squishy.

🥛

5. Glue

Brush on the white glue.

✨

6. Wax

Cover it with wax and let it dry.

🫧

7. Squeeze!

Finally, squeeze your finished squishy!
